What is Opposite Match?
👧🧒

Designed for children aged 5 to 8

A fun, safe, and educational vocabulary game where children learn English antonyms (opposites) through guided matching gameplay. Built with children's privacy and safety as the top priority — no ads, no tracking, no internet required.
